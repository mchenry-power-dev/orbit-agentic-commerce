# Brief control coverage

This map describes the no-key catalog, composition and local handoff workflow. [StudioBrief](../src/domain/studio.ts), [forms](../src/app/StudioForms.tsx), [local interpretation](../src/fixtures/studio.ts), [service request mapping](../src/providers/service-client.ts), [checks](../src/validation/placements.ts) and [handoff](../src/export/studio.ts) are the source of truth. Service mappings describe preserved deferred integrations, not hosted model capabilities. Structural coverage is not evidence that an arbitrary semantic instruction was understood.

Direction editors cover the direction name, scene notes, palette, visual mode, art direction, product scope, framing and placement headline. The selected-variant editor exposes explicit composition controls and recomposes only that output. Names identify families. Palette/mode/art-direction/framing and explicit variant overrides enter real composition recipes; placement headlines appear on website/email overlays while Google images keep ad copy separate. Local scene text remains review context rather than arbitrary scene synthesis. In live mode its edited text becomes the scene prompt. Edits require renewed confirmation and invalidate affected dependencies. Structured per-placement composition and spacing enter actual recipes; descriptive framing/focal/negative-space notes remain review context. Equal-size variants with different layout choices remain separate.

Merchant feedback/next-iteration notes stay in the brief and can enter a new interpretation. Local supported cues inspect those notes; the configured semantic request transmits feedback separately from the campaign description, with each field bounded to 6,000 characters. Current instructions, confirmed product facts and complete phrase requirements stay whole within their bounds or fail validation. Public-source reference text may be explicitly marked as an excerpt of at most 2,000 characters per reference, including the marker; its complete sanitized text remains in the local source record. Visitor text without a public URL is not silently shortened. These are context limits, not evidence of live semantic relevance.

Comma/newline inputs retain raw editing text alongside parsed arrays, rather than rewriting the field while typing. New-campaign and existing-campaign drafts are isolated from confirmed plans. Empty numeric text maps to `null`; zero maps to 0. [Studio tests](../tests/studio.test.ts) cover supported interpretation, whole phrases, zero/blank budgets, draft cleanup, placement layouts, targeted revision recovery and exact exports. Desktop/narrow browser assertions cover input retention, immediate planning, navigation and draft isolation; their scope is recorded in the [testing guide](testing-and-evaluation.md).

The hosted local composer does not make every free-text goal, audience or tone produce a new semantic creative idea. It exposes supported festive, cool and editorial interpretation for review, with editable palette, framing, spacing and copy. Scene notes remain human review context. Full semantic application is deferred; it would require an authorized provider and real acceptance campaigns, which mocked contracts cannot establish. See the [capability boundary](provider-and-runtime.md).
